# PocketAuthor Community Code of Conduct

PocketAuthor is building tools and public resources for authors, publishing professionals, researchers, creators, and contributors around the world.

We want collaboration here to be constructive, rigorous, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes rather than attacking people.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and clarity.
- Be patient with contributors from different technical, professional, linguistic, and cultural backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, authorship, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Give credit for other people's work.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, stalking, intimidation, deliberate disruption, plagiarism, impersonation, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Do not pressure contributors to disclose private manuscripts, identities, credentials, personal history, or other information unrelated to the work.

## Publishing-specific expectations

Because PocketAuthor works with creative and intellectual work:

- do not upload manuscripts or excerpts you do not have the right to share;
- do not misrepresent AI-generated, copied, translated, or adapted work as someone else's original authorship;
- do not remove attribution or provenance to conceal a source;
- do not use project channels to solicit infringement or evasion of lawful rights.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community or project.

If you need to report conduct privately, use a non-public project contact channel when one is available. For security issues, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code applies to PocketAuthor-controlled GitHub spaces and project interactions.
